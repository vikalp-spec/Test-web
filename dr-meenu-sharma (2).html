About Dr. Meenu Sharma

12+ Years of Healing Lives in Noida

Dr. Meenu Sharma is a distinguished RCI (Rehabilitation Council of India) Certified Clinical Psychologist based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. With over 12 years of dedicated clinical practice, she has helped thousands of individuals overcome mental health challenges and lead fulfilling lives.

Holding a Ph.D. in Psychology, Dr. Sharma specializes in evidence-based therapeutic approaches including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Mindfulness-Based Therapy, and Solution-Focused Brief Therapy. Her warm, non-judgmental approach creates a safe space for clients of all ages.
